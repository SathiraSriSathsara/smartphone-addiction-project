Validation method and results

The executed workflow used a stratified 80/20 train-validation split with random state 42 and three-fold stratified cross-validation with shuffling.

Limitations

  • The competition dataset may not represent every population, culture, age group or context.
  • Self-reported inputs can be approximate or biased.
  • Probability is not clinical certainty or proof of addiction.
  • Risk bands are display thresholds and are not clinically validated.
  • Explanation factors describe model influence, not causes.
  • Performance can change when real-world data differs from training data.

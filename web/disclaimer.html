Please read before using SmartHabit

Use with perspective

SmartHabit is a university research and educational prototype. Its output should support reflection only.

Not a medical diagnosis

SmartHabit does not diagnose smartphone addiction or any medical or psychological condition. Its output should not guide healthcare decisions, replace a qualified professional or label a person as medically addicted.

Educational and research use

The application demonstrates a computational-intelligence workflow for CIS6005.

Intended use

Explore how usage patterns are processed by a competition model and use the result for reflection.

Not intended for

Screening, diagnosis, treatment or decisions affecting education, employment, insurance or access to services.

Interpret responsibly

A predicted class or probability is a model estimate, not a statement of fact.

Privacy statement

The form requests only model inputs and excludes the Kaggle ID. The current application sends fields to the prediction API and has no user accounts or application database for retaining submissions.

Do not enter identifying information. A deployed server or hosting provider may keep technical access logs under its own policy; check the applicable deployment policy before submitting sensitive information.

Known limitations

  • The competition data may not represent an individual or every population.
  • Inputs may be estimates rather than measurements.
  • Dataset bias, missing context and changing behaviour can affect results.
  • Risk bands are application display thresholds, not clinical thresholds.
  • Explanation factors are model associations, not causes.

Professional and emergency support

This page does not provide medical advice. If smartphone use or wellbeing is causing concern, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare or mental-health professional. If you believe there is an immediate risk of harm or an emergency, contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted emergency resource in your area.
